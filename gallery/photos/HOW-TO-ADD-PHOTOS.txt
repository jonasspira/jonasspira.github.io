HOW TO ADD YOUR PHOTOS
======================
1. Go to github.com/jonasspira/jonasspira.github.io  ->  gallery/photos/
2. Click "Add file" -> "Upload files", then drag your photo files in.
3. Click "Commit changes".
That's it. The gallery page updates itself within ~1 minute. No code editing.

- Supported: .jpg .jpeg .png .gif .webp  (and .svg)
- Photos appear in alphabetical order by filename. To control the order,
  prefix names like 01-beach.jpg, 02-sunset.jpg, 03-...
- Delete the sample-01.svg ... sample-06.svg files once you've added real photos.
