MI Plates

Michigan seems to have more custom vanity plates per capita than just about anywhere — and it isn't close.

Theory: Michigan's Secretary of State form has a line for personalizing a plate right there on the page. While plenty of states offer vanity plates, when you have to ask for them, fewer people do.

A growing collection spotted around the state, June 2024 to today — about 200 plates so far, with more added all the time.
